## Description

The largest island in the [[Geography/Kalantawi Islands/Kalantawi_Islands|Kalantawi Islands]], forming the western end of the chain. Densely forested across its northern interior, with a mountain range running along its southern coastline. On the [[Maps/Estevaterra_World_Map|Estevaterra World Map]] it's the westernmost of the chain's landmasses — the one carrying the "KINGDOM" portion of the Kingdom of Yahan label — and it isn't yet distinguished from the rest of the chain by its own label or border on that map.
