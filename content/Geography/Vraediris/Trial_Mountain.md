# ⛰️ The Trial Mountain

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Type:** Mountain
- **Size/Scale:** Tall enough for a multi-stage switchback trail; peak reaches a waterfall-fed cavern
- **Climate:** Shaded from the worst of the desert heat — the mountain itself blocks the sun for most of the ascent

## Description

Barren of visible life, but for a waterfall near the peak that appears to vanish before reaching the base — its water is later found pouring into a cavern partway up rather than continuing down the slope. A switchback trail is the only apparent way up.

Likely part of the same range visible from the desert as "large hills leading southwest to a mountain range" and the canyon route described in [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_5|Act 5]] — not explicitly confirmed as the same mountain the ruins are set into, though both are described as reached via the same general southwestern range.

Guarded partway up by three [[Bestiary/Lamia|Lamia]], who use _Disguise Self_ to impersonate Leonin, _Major Image_ to hide a genuine trail collapse, and rockfalls to defend the peak — see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_6]] for the full trial sequence.

## Inhabitants

- 3 [[Bestiary/Lamia|Lamia]], guarding the upper trail and the peak's waterfall cavern.

## History

The site of the Leonin's own coming-of-age trial — every Leonin must reach the peak and return with water from the cavern spring, alone, unassisted, or be considered too weak for the tribe. The party undergoes the same trial in [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_6]] to earn the location of the ruins believed to hold [[Magic/Items/Luminous_Sphere_of_Eldrida|the Luminous Sphere of Eldrida]].
