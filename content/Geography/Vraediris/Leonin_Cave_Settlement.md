# 📍 The Leonin Cave Settlement

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Type:** Hidden cave settlement / headquarters
- **Settlement:** N/A — home to [[Factions/Leonin|the Leonin]], not a settlement in the Settlements/ sense
- **District:** N/A
- **Political Division:** N/A — [[Vraediris]] has no stated political division

## Description

Reached from the desert surface via winding rock passages — cooler and damper than the desert above, sand giving way to solid stone underfoot. Entered blindfolded by any party the Leonin capture, so its exact position relative to the landing beach or the trial mountain isn't established.

**Known areas:**

- **A prison cell:** a bare cave-like room with a barred opening, a couple of large flat stones for sitting or lying on, no other furnishings. Guarded by two Leonin at the gate.
- **A storage room:** holds confiscated party gear; pointed out by a rescued child after the Eldrokai invasion if the party still needs their equipment.
- **Multiple further rooms**, not individually named, where the [[Factions/Eldrokai|Eldrokai]] invasion plays out room by room — see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_6]]'s Invasion Encounters.

Home to Leonin families, not just warriors — children are seen throughout, curious about (or wary of) the prisoners depending on their features.

## Inhabitants

- [[Factions/Leonin|The Leonin]], specifically the Golden Sands tribe under [[Characters/NPCs/Kaela|Kaela]].
- Leonin children, unnamed.

## History

Site of the party's capture, imprisonment, and the [[Factions/Eldrokai|Eldrokai]] invasion that leads to their being offered the tribe's water trial — see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_6]] for the full sequence.
