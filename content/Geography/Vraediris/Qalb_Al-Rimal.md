## Description

An ancient cave-city carved into a mountain face, reached via a two-night desert journey and a canyon approach from [[Vraediris|the Isle of Vraediris]]. A battered outer wall and gate front the mountain, beyond which lies a vast cave interior subdivided by several more layers of walls — how deep the city goes isn't clear from outside. Now a ruin, fought over by rival factions and fiends (hobgoblins, a fire giant stronghold, a cyclops tribe, desert howlers, and reportedly a blue dragon) drawn by the lingering power of the city's old, pre-magic-age magic.

## History

Reached by [[Characters/NPCs/Akemi]] guiding the party over two nights of desert travel following [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_6|Act 6]]'s water trial, navigating by starlight (the constellation Draco) and surviving an Eldrokai ambush (FtK26) and a Laiqamorian mercenary ambush led by [[Characters/NPCs/Norjor_Fogflow]] (FtK27) along the way. In ancient times the city's own gods were cast out once its people learned to wield magic themselves. **Confirmed by Shane: [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Avaretal|Avaretal]], Empress of Fire, is the real Old God tied to this city** — Qalb Al-Rimal fell within her canonical lands in Estevaterra, though the full extent of her domain elsewhere in the world is still to be mapped out once Shane divides the world among the pantheon; [[Pantheon/Gods_of_Qalb_Al-Rimal]] was an early, superseded draft and no longer reflects the city's real local lore. Why the city itself was eventually abandoned is a separate open question in Shane's own notes: desert hardship, or the predators and dangerous magical beings (ancestors of today's giants) that couldn't be kept at bay.

## Designer Notes

**Seven of the city's districts are built out so far** — City Gates, Old Marketplace, the Hobgoblin Stronghold (Residential SW), the Garden, the Industrial district, the Hobgoblin Rebels camp (Residential E), and the Government district (Residential NE — never actually a cyclops stronghold, despite an earlier planning note guessing otherwise; Fire Giants fighting [[Characters/NPCs/Benedict_Glorybluff|Commander Glorybluff's]] brigade is its real content), all from [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_8]]. The remaining districts are named and outlined in Shane's own planning notes but not yet written as scenes: the Fire Giant stronghold (on the city's west side, per the Hobgoblin Rebels' own scouting — though the Giants also now appear to have pushed into the Government district itself; where Commander Glorybluff's rescue very likely already played out, and whose "books" turned out to live in the Government district's own secret library), the Palace (the blue dragon and the Luminous Sphere decision — beyond the Fire Giants' territory, per the Hobgoblin Rebels' own intel), and an uncertain "Caves(?)" area with no further detail given. See [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_8]]'s Designer Notes for the full roadmap.
