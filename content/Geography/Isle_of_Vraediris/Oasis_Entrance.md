# 🌴 The Oasis Entrance

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Type:** Desert oasis
- **Size/Scale:** A small pond, ringed by palm trees
- **Climate:** Notably cooler and more pleasant than the surrounding desert

## Description

> _The air gets thicker with the scent of blooming desert flowers as you arrive at the oasis. The sound of trickling water is a welcome relief to your parched throats. Tall palm trees surround a small pond of clear water, with soft sand and smooth rocks forming a beach along its edge. The sun beats down from overhead, casting a bright glare across the area. There are no other inhabitants in sight, and the only sounds are the occasional bird chirping or insect buzzing nearby. The oasis seems peaceful and inviting, with no immediate signs of current or former civilization._

Marked by large cacti while still approaching, per [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_5|Act 5]]'s route description. No sign of current or former civilization on arrival, despite Glorybluff's hope that an old colony might have settled here.

Whichever destination the party reaches — this one included — the same ambush by [[Factions/Leonin|the Leonin]] follows shortly after arrival.
