## Description

> _Climbing over the last dune you see a break in the landscape before you. The massive sand dunes are replaced by rockier ground rising into massive, sand covered hills that cover the land for miles before the steep mountains in the distance. The hot air begins to cool as you cross to the solid ground. The hills are devoid of any life or vegetation, but there is something strange about them. The rock formations sprouting from them seem unnaturally linear._

Notably barren of life or vegetation, unlike the oasis. The "unnaturally linear" rock formations are noted but not explained in the source — possibly a hint of old ruins or construction, not otherwise detailed.

Whichever destination the party reaches — this one included — the same ambush by [[Factions/Leonin|the Leonin]] follows shortly after arrival.
