# 🏜️ The Canyon Entrance

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Type:** Canyon, possibly an old riverbed
- **Size/Scale:** _(not stated)_
- **Climate:** Shaded relief from the desert sun, per its towering walls

## Description

> _You approach the canyon (slick with sweat, exhausted and slightly dehydrated from the desert journey) and its towering walls provide welcome shade from the brutal sun. The sand here is different than the open desert, redder and more subdued with no large dunes to navigate. You can see several rocks and other objects scattered in the distance in the canyon before you._

Cut between the mountains, per [[Characters/NPCs/Benedict_Glorybluff|Glorybluff]]'s own guess possibly an old riverbed once used by whatever civilization built the ruins the party seeks. Distinct reddish sand, free of the open desert's large dunes. This is also the route that continues on toward the mountain ruins believed to hold [[Magic/Items/Luminous_Sphere_of_Eldrida|the Luminous Sphere of Eldrida]] — see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_5]]'s "What Guards the Sphere."

Whichever destination the party reaches — this one included — the same ambush by [[Factions/Leonin|the Leonin]] follows shortly after arrival.
