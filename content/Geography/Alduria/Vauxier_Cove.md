## Description

A cove near [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Aprea/Bangola/Bangola|Bangola]] hosting the city's larger, deeper-water port — used by bigger ships like [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Aprea/Bangola/The_Ashanti|The Ashanti]] — as distinct from Bangola's own river port. The port itself is unnamed in the source and treated as part of the city rather than its own location. In the deeps off the cove lies [[Geography/Alduria/Nauticus_Bay|Nauticus Bay]], the hidden undersea city of [[Factions/Merfolk_of_Nauticus_Bay|its merfolk]], who resent large ships that use the river route through Bangola instead of the cove's own deeper water.
