## Description

A landmass in the southern portion of Estevaterra, between the Tisver and Montaumart Oceans, roughly south of [[Political_Divisions/Laiqamore|Laiqamore]] and [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Kingdom_of_Yeripia|Yeripia]]. Arid, tan-colored terrain across its northern reaches gives way to a dark, jagged mountain range running along its southern coast.
