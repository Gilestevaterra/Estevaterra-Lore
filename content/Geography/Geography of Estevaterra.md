# ⛰️ Land Masses

Estevaterra is made up of 2 large land masses which touch at a singular point, and several islands in the south. The eastern continent is [[Alduria]], rich in farmlands and protected by mountain ranges on each coast. The western continent is [[Bezmoria]], much fouled with marshes and swamps in the center and arid desert to the far west.
