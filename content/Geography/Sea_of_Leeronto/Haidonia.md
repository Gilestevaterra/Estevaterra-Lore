## Description

A small volcanic landmass — red-black terrain with an active-looking peak — sitting in the Sea of Leeronto between [[Political_Divisions/Laiqamore|Laiqamore]] and [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Kingdom_of_Yeripia|Yeripia]], just south of Santerel Deep. _(Confirmed by Shane, 2026-09-07: the Kingdom of Yeripia regional map shows a fragment of Haidonia's terrain at its western edge, near Yeripia's [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Swauz_Glon/Swauz_Glon|Swauz Glon]] coast — placing Haidonia just offshore there.)_
