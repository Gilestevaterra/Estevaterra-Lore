Regional cutout of the [[Maps/Estevaterra_World_Map|Estevaterra World Map]], blown up with added county borders (the dotted white lines are provincial boundaries, not roads — confirmed by Shane, 2026-09-07) and local detail: settlements, lakes, and rivers not visible at world scale. The settlement spelled "Kisechov" here differs from what the vault originally had, "Kiseshov" — confirmed by Shane as a vault typo; the page and filename have both been corrected. A handful of smaller unlabeled streams east of Withergate/Utrye and a partial river name near the coast were visible but not confirmed; not represented as their own pages yet.
