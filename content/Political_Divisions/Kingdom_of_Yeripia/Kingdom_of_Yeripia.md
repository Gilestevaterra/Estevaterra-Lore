# 🗺️ Kingdom of Yeripia

## At a Glance

- **Level:** Kingdom
- **Government Type:** Hereditary monarchy, with a peerage of Duke/Duchess (province) → Earl/Count (county) → Baron (barony) → Mayor (self-governing city), plus a Marquess as a province's military leader and a Viscount handling a county's justice and taxes.
- **Ruler/Leader:** [[Characters/NPCs/Rawwin_Venbalar]] (King) and [[Characters/NPCs/Ismeryll_Venbalar]] (Queen, née Sylkas), seated at [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Dushua/Egnatia/Warlton_Palace]] in [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Dushua/Egnatia/Egnatia]]
- **Dominant Faction:** [[Factions/]]

## Description

**Yeripia has 7 provinces: Preopia, Dushua, Aprea, Swauz Glon, Tiket, Utrye, and Mathium.**

Yeripia has been at war with [[Political_Divisions/Laiqamore]] for as long as anyone can remember.
