## Description

Occupies the western end of the same continent as [[Political_Divisions/Laiqamore|Laiqamore]] — the two share one landmass rather than being separated by open water. Arid, tan-colored terrain with scattered mountains, in contrast to Laiqamore's greener country to its east. The Rigesonne Depths lie off its western coast, along with a scatter of small islands and, further southwest, the [[Slaecaea_Islands|Slaecaea Islands]].
