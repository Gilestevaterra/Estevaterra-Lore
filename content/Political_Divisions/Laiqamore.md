_(Still no physical description or detailed geography given, but its ruler and current motive are now on record via King Rawwin Venbalar's account, see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_2]].)_

Everything above is the Yeripian/King Rawwin side of the conflict, genuinely believed as truth within Yeripia (see [[Modules/For_the_Kingdom/For_the_Kingdom_Act_4]]). [[Characters/NPCs/Kerthri]], a captured Laiqamorian spy, gives Laiqamore's own mirror-image account once caught: that Laiqamore is poor and run-down rather than the threat Yeripians believe it to be, and that it's actually Yeripia that raids and plunders. Neither side's account is objective in-world fact — each kingdom's people genuinely believe their own version is the truth. Recorded below as Laiqamore's own belief about itself and about Yeripia, not as settled history.
