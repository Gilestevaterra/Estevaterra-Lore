# 🗺️ Laiqamore

## At a Glance

- **Level:** Kingdom
- **Government Type:** Designated Monarchy
- **Ruler/Leader:** [[Characters/NPCs/Zarek|Zarek "the Tyrant"]]
- **Dominant Faction:** [[Factions/]]

## Description

Laiqamore has a very industrial feel to it. Its lands are a combination of hills and mountains used for mining and swamps where it is difficult to eke out a living. This creates hard people who have strong work ethics, and opinions of those who do not.

Laiqamore has been at war with the [[Kingdom_of_Yeripia]] for as long as anyone can remember, though it is closer to a cold war without many major battles. Only skirmishes across the isthmus connecting their continents.
