# 🏛️ Pantheon Overview

The Old Gods created Estevaterra. Each held domain over an element (or an elemental concept, in the case of Life and Death) and ruled directly over a physical part of the world, in addition to that domain. They were not inherently "good" or "evil" in any conventional sense, they simply were. As such they were seen by different people as saviors or as cruel despots depending on where those people stood on various socio political issues. In the time of their rule, magic did not exist except as something the gods doled out directly: fantastical objects and creatures were their creations alone, and such creation was rare.&#x20;

With the disappearance of the Old Gods, New Gods now oversee Estevaterra. They take a more benign approach than the Old Gods and rarely directly interact with mortal affairs. Instead they choose to guide and inspire people to achieve their desires.

## The New Gods

There are 8 New Gods in the Estevaterra Pantheon

- [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Aradia]] (Necromancy)
- [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Dheerin]] (Transmutation)
- [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Dophy]] (Divination)
- [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Galaphorus]] (Enchantment)
- [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Kogeros]] (Evocation)
- [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Solum]] (Conjuration)
- [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Valtor]] (Abjuration)
- [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Xyzdia]] (Illusion)

While each god has their own distinct personality and interests, they are all largely considered to be "good" and do not run the gamut of traditional alignment ranges. Those who worship chaos and evil would be following various demons, devils and other cult leaders who may profess to be gods but are not. The Gods do not typically take an active role in the everyday lives of the people of Estevaterra, and instead follow the mantra that if they are doing their jobs correctly you cannot tell if they have done anything at all.
