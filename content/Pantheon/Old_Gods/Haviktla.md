# ✨ Haviktla

_Mistress of the Depths_

## Identity

- **Generation:** Old God
- **Domain(s):** Water
- **Symbol:** Three overlapping wave crests in a spiral.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Haviktla looks like a cross between a giant squid and an old dragon skeleton, nearly a hundred feet long. Her scales shift color like oil on dark water. Black volcanic glass covers her torso like armor. She has four webbed arms, each ending in claws of sharp blue coral. Her head is shaped like a shark's skull, with six eyes, three to a side, glowing pale green. A mass of thick tentacles hangs off the back of her head, each one tipped with a small snapping beak. Her body narrows into an eel-like tail ending in a wide, see-through fin. Thin fins run down her spine and vibrate to make the low sounds fishermen call the songs of the deep.
