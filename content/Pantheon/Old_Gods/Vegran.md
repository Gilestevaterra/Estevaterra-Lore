# ✨ Vegran

_Lord of the Undead_
**Status:** #draft / #reviewed

## Identity

- **Generation:** Old God
- **Domain(s):** Death
- **Symbol:** A shining soul-flame trapped inside a ribcage.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Vegran is a massive skeletal serpent-centipede. A stag skull with too many horns, carved from black fossilized ivory, sits where his head should be. His eye sockets hold twin whirls of violet soul-fire instead of eyes. His body is one long ribcage that never seems to end, and thousands of small blue lanterns hang inside it off his spine, each one holding a soul. Hundreds of blade-legs made of iron and bone run down his sides, clicking against the ground like dry leaves. A torn black funeral shroud trails out behind him.

## Personality & Nature

Vegran is actively seeking a way to return fully to the world. He works through mortal agents, speaking to and manipulating individuals directly rather than relying solely on organized worship, granting them power or revealing hidden truths in exchange for serving his return. "Missing in Lostshade" is the first installment in a planned series of modules tracking this larger scheme. Hadrian Fenwick is the first such agent.

[[Characters/NPCs/Benevolence_Mistsplitter]], a young vampire spawn met in [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Preopia/Gidor_County/Hallenfeld/Hallenfeld_Meeting_Benevolence_Overview|Meeting Benevolence]], is a second instance of this pattern. She states she worships no god, yet speaks fondly during that encounter of "Her Lord," and says he is coming for them all. That Lord is Vegran, echoing Hadrian Fenwick's role as a mortal agent rather than a worshipper. This stays subtle in-game: information a party may or may not pick up on rather than an outright reveal.

## Territory

Vegran ruled over the western half of [[Geography/Alduria/Alduria]]: the portion drained by [[Geography/Alduria/Fjelling_Lake]] and [[Geography/Alduria/Ostavia_River]], bounded by the [[Geography/Alduria/Glenmara_Mountains]] and, to the east, the [[Geography/Alduria/Dunbrayne_Foothills|Dunbrayne Foothills]], where his lands meet Mercaraz's.
