# ✨ Avaretal

_Empress of Fire_
**Status:** #draft / #reviewed

## Identity

- **Generation:** Old God
- **Domain(s):** Fire
- **Symbol:** A flaming sun with a hollow center.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Avaretal has no solid body, she is fire itself. Her shape is a tall column of flame that keeps twisting back on itself, never holding one form for long. A ball of black obsidian hovers at her center, pulsing red like a heartbeat. When she needs limbs, they burst out of the flame and melt back in once she's done with them. A ring of gold hoops floats above her head, shedding a steady rain of sparks. The heat rolling off her keeps the air around her hazy.

## Personality & Nature

Volatile and quick to burn through her own moods, calm one moment and raging the next. Values boldness and action over caution, and has little patience for hesitation or excuses. Creates as easily as she destroys, a forge fire that shapes metal is still her fire, and she doesn't see much difference between building something and burning it down. Demands worship through deeds rather than words, and shows favor by testing people with trials of nerve or endurance.

## Worshippers

- **Religion/Church:** [[Pantheon/Religions/The_Ember_Covenant|The Ember Covenant]] _(defunct, isolated remnant worship survives)_
- **Cults:** [[Pantheon/Cults/The_Cinderborn|The Cinderborn]], [[Pantheon/Cults/Emberkeepers_of_the_Fallen_Throne|Emberkeepers of the Fallen Throne]]

## Territory

Avaretal ruled over the [[Slaecaea_Islands|Slaecaea Islands]], the [[Vraediris|Isle of Vraediris]], [[Geography/Sea_of_Leeronto/Haidonia|Haidonia]], and the small islands scattered through the [[Geography/Tisver_Ocean/Tisver_Ocean|Tisver Ocean]], the [[Geography/Sea_of_Leeronto/Sea_of_Leeronto|Sea of Leeronto]], and the [[Geography/Tisver_Ocean/Rigesonne_Depths|Rigesonne Depths]]. [[Qalb_Al-Rimal]], on the Isle of Vraediris, was the seat of her power.
