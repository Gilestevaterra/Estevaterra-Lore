# ✨ Avaretal

_Empress of Fire_

## Identity

- **Generation:** Old God
- **Domain(s):** Fire
- **Symbol:** A flaming sun with a hollow center.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Avaretal has no solid body, she is fire itself. Her shape is a tall column of flame that keeps twisting back on itself, never holding one form for long. A ball of black obsidian hovers at her center, pulsing red like a heartbeat. When she needs limbs, they burst out of the flame and melt back in once she's done with them. A ring of gold hoops floats above her head, shedding a steady rain of sparks. The heat rolling off her keeps the air around her hazy.
