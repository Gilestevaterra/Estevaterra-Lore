# ✨ Faranaha

_The Life Mother_
**Status:** #draft / #reviewed

## Identity

- **Generation:** Old God
- **Domain(s):** Life
- **Symbol:** A sprouting seed inside a perfect circle.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Faranaha is a huge floating tree, wrapped in a shell of hardened amber bark. She drifts slowly through the sky, glowing faintly like a giant seed. A heart made of woven silver roots beats at the center of her trunk, pulsing with a soft green light, and every beat sends a wave of growth through the ground below her. Thin silver-white vines run out from the heart and fill the branches, blooming year-round with flowers that shouldn't grow side by side: arctic poppies, desert cacti, kelp from the deep sea. She doesn't speak. Instead she breathes out a golden mist heavy with pollen, smelling of rain and crushed mint, and anyone it touches can hear her thoughts.

## Personality & Nature

Patient and slow to act, closer to a season changing than a person making a decision. Values growth in any form it takes, more like a gardener than a judge, and doesn't rank one kind of life above another. Has little use for cruelty or waste: killing something and leaving it to rot offends her more than killing it for a reason. Rarely intervenes directly, letting things grow or die on their own timeline, and speaks to mortals only through the pollen mist rather than words.
