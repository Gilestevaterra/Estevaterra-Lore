# ✨ Kogeros

_The Catalyst_

## Identity

- **Generation:** New God
- **Domain(s):** Evocation
- **Symbol:** A closed fist radiating jagged energy lines.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Kogeros looks like an empty suit of scorched black plate armor. There's no body inside it, just a core of white arcane light that bleeds out through the joints and the visor. A star is engraved on her breastplate, glowing softly. Her gauntlets never actually touch her arms, they hover, held in place by arcs of white light. In place of a cloak she wears living fire. She carries a staff made of frozen lightning, and it doesn't just glow, it hums, a low vibration you can feel more than hear.

## Personality & Nature

Direct, forceful, and impatient with anyone who hoards power without using it. Values ambition and progress over caution: she was the first of the New Gods to figure out how to take magic from the Old Gods rather than wait to be given it, and she still thinks that way. Has little patience for tradition for its own sake, seeing it as another Old God habit worth burning away. Mortals who worship her describe a lingering heat to her presence, as if the fire she once was hasn't fully gone out.
