# ✨ Valtor

_The Unbroken_

## Identity

- **Generation:** New God
- **Domain(s):** Abjuration
- **Symbol:** A large hexagonal shield with a thick border.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Valtor is a massive warrior, close to fifteen feet tall, built out of shifting geometric plates. His skin is his armor: thousands of interlocking hexagon tiles that ripple like water when struck, scattering the force so nothing pierces or dents him. Twelve floating tower shields, made of pure force, orbit around him like a crown and move on their own to block anything coming at him before it lands. His hands are oversized, and his palms glow with a bright white sigil. He wears a heavy surcoat of woven cold iron covered in runes, so heavy it never moves in the wind, like it's anchored straight to the ground.

## Personality & Nature

Steady and unshakeable, closer to a wall than a person in temperament. Values defense over conquest, and measures strength by what a person can endure and protect rather than destroy. Doesn't seek glory, content to simply hold a line, and expects the same quiet resolve from anyone who calls on him. Has no patience for cowardice dressed up as caution, but draws a clear line between running from a fight and standing your ground to protect something worth protecting.

## Worshippers

- **Religion/Church:** [[Pantheon/Religions/The_Unbroken_Line|The Unbroken Line]]
- **Cults:** [[Pantheon/Cults/]]

## Origins

Before he was Valtor, he was Bogdan Kamenov of Laiqamore, and his casting against [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Mgellzir]] was the hardest of the six. Mgellzir's flesh was closer to living stone than anything the Riftspike had been driven into before, and Bogdan spent longer under the Old God's blows than any caster before or after him, absorbing punishment that would have killed an ordinary man outright. Whatever the transformation carried forward from that ordeal, it left Valtor built never to take a hit so directly again: his current form scatters force across a thousand shifting plates rather than simply enduring it, as if some part of him is still bracing for a blow that never quite lands the same way twice.
