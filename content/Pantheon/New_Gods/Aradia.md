# ✨ Aradia

_The Pale Master_

## Identity

- **Generation:** New God
- **Domain(s):** Necromancy
- **Symbol:** A human skull resting on an open book.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Aradia looks like a skeletal aristocrat, his bones polished smooth and white as marble, with no rot or decay anywhere on him. Fine necromantic runes are etched into the bone in silver ink. Where his organs should be, a lattice of pale smoke hangs in the shape of a heart and lungs, pulsing with a cold blue light. His fingers are long, too many joints in each one, tipped in sharp obsidian points, and he moves them constantly like he's plucking harp strings, though what he's really touching are the threads tying the dead nearby to this world. He wears a high-collared gown of solid shadow that moves like liquid, its hem never quite touching the ground, trailing off into mist as he walks. A circlet of frozen flame hovers just above his brow, glowing faint green.

## Personality & Nature

Calm and controlled, and treats death less as an ending and more as a raw material to be worked with care. Values precision and permanence: nothing about him decays or falls out of order, and he expects the same discipline from anyone who serves him. Not cruel so much as detached, more interested in a soul's craftsmanship than a mortal's suffering. Holds the dead close in a proprietary way and doesn't take well to anyone disturbing graves or souls he considers his to manage.
