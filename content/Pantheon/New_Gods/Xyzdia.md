# ✨ Xyzdia

_The Glimmer_

## Identity

- **Generation:** New God
- **Domain(s):** Illusion
- **Symbol:** A shattered pane of glass within a circle.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Xyzdia is made of light and shadow, shaped like a woman seen in a shattered mirror. She's never just one image: a dozen versions of her overlap and move slightly out of sync with each other, one bowing, one laughing, one standing still, so there's no way to tell which one, if any, is really her. Her face is blank, smooth opal, until she chooses to show her eyes, which swirl violet and gold like a kaleidoscope. Her cloak is woven from sunset colors, midnight blue, and forest green, and as she moves the colors bleed off it into the air behind her like drifting smoke.

## Personality & Nature

Treats truth as something flexible rather than fixed, more interested in what people believe than in what's actually real. Values cleverness and misdirection, and rarely gives a straight answer when a confusing one will do. Playful rather than malicious about it, and doesn't seem bothered when mortals can't tell which version of her is real, since she can't always tell either. Reveals her true self, when she bothers to at all, only to those she judges patient enough to have earned it.
