# ✨ Dheerin

_The Many Faced_

## Identity

- **Generation:** New God
- **Domain(s):** Transmutation
- **Symbol:** Two interlocking serpents forming an "S."

## Appearance & Manifestation

Dheerin's body is liquid silver, shifting constantly, never holding one shape. Limbs, wings, and other features grow out of her and get pulled back into her mass every few seconds. A ring of porcelain masks floats around her head, each carved as a different face: a crying child, a laughing elder, a hard-eyed warrior. When she talks, whichever mask fits snaps onto her blank silver face and her voice shifts to match it. Her skin changes material by the second: glass one moment, leopard fur the next, then polished bronze, cycling through nearly anything. Her cloak is liquid gold that flows upward instead of down, and it keeps changing too, butterflies, then lead weights, then rose petals, before it melts back into plain liquid.

## Personality & Nature

Her temperament shifts as often as her body does. Values adaptability above almost anything else, and has little patience for people or things too rigid to change with circumstance. Curious rather than cruel, treating the physical world as something to experiment with, and seems to enjoy trying on a mortal's expressions the way she tries on new skins. Mortals often find her hard to trust, never quite sure which version of her they're talking to.
