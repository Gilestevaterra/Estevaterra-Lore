# ✨ Solum

_The Gate Queen_
**Status:** #draft / #reviewed

## Identity

- **Generation:** New God
- **Domain(s):** Conjuration
- **Symbol:** A keyhole shaped like a swirling vortex.

## Appearance & Manifestation

Solum is a huge shape, folded out of space itself, and hard to see clearly for more than a moment. At her core she looks like a tall, regal woman with skin dotted like a starry sky. Behind her two main arms, hundreds of see-through hands reach out from rifts in the air, each one busy, each one vanishing into some other world. Instead of a crown, a small black hole hovers above her head, ringed by old stones that slowly turn around it. Sometimes small creatures from other planes peek out through it. Wherever she walks, the hem of her dress leaves behind little patches of other realities: a stretch of desert sand here, a splash of seawater there. A ring of crystal keys hangs from her waist, each one humming with the note of a different plane.

## Personality & Nature

Thinks in doors and thresholds rather than fixed places, and doesn't stay anywhere, including one shape, for very long. Values access and possibility over permanence, endlessly curious about what's on the other side of every gate she opens. Isn't possessive of any one world, which makes her hard to bargain with: she has nowhere she truly calls home, so it costs her little to close a door on someone. Demands keys and open passageways kept in her name rather than temples built to stay in one place.

## Worshippers

## Origins

Before she was Solum, she was Beatriz Windgate of Breotrasseon's Portuguese-speaking coast, and she was the one who actually drove the Riftspike into Mercaraz while Rodrigo Charmwind held the god's wound open beside her. She remembers the instant the rift opened better than anything else about that day: not Mercaraz being pulled through it, but the glimpse of what was on the other side, gone again before she could name it. Chasing that glimpse, and the doors it might still be hiding, is most of what Solum has done with her godhood since.
