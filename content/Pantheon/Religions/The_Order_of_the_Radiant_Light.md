## Core Beliefs

Certainty is the real heresy. A worshipper who thinks they've finally seen Xyzdia clearly has simply been shown what she wanted them to see.

## Practices & Rituals

A Radiant Light service is as much performance as prayer, built around dazzling illusions of light meant to make the truth behind them impossible to pin down. The Order takes its name, and its flagship temple, from the same shimmering light-tricks that give Xyzdia her own name among mortals.

## Structure & Hierarchy

Smaller shrines and lone devotees turn up wherever a stage performer or a confidence artist wants her blessing, with no need to ever set foot in Egnatia. The Temple of the Radiant Light remains the faith's actual seat and its most organized chapter.
