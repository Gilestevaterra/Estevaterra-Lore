## Core Beliefs

Strength is measured by what you protect and endure, not what you conquer. Cowardice dressed up as caution earns no respect, but there's a clear line between running from a fight and standing your ground to protect something worth protecting.

## Practices & Rituals

Line initiates train to hold a position under sustained pressure rather than to strike first. A Line shield-wall is considered a shrine in its own right for as long as it holds, wherever it happens to be raised.

## Structure & Hierarchy

Members are guards, soldiers, and militia who take Valtor's own creed as a personal oath. The faith keeps little formal hierarchy beyond whoever's held the line longest, which has let it travel easily well past Laiqamore's own borders.
