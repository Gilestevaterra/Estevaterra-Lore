# ⛪ The Silver Skein

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Deity/Deities Worshipped:** [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Dophy]]
- **Type:** Oracular Faith
- **Size:** Small, with independent diviner circles well beyond Crucaea
- **Headquarters:** a fortune-hall somewhere in [[Political_Divisions/Crucaea|Crucaea]], her own homeland _(open, exact site unconfirmed)_

## Core Beliefs

Knowing what's coming and being fit to meet it are the same kind of care. Every reading that isn't good news is a real loss, worth carrying rather than delivering coldly.

## Practices & Rituals

The Skein reads fortunes the way Dophy herself reads a life's thread. Skein diviners are trained to treat every reading as a genuine loss when it isn't good news, the same weight Dophy herself is said to carry for every thread she cuts.

## Structure & Hierarchy

Keeps its deepest roots in Crucaea, where Dophy was born Meera Suncaller. Its circles elsewhere have taken on some unexpected shapes: the healers at Egnatia's [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Dushua/Egnatia/The_Silver_Pinnacle]] double as one such circle, an odd pairing the Order has stopped trying to explain and simply practices: a Skein reading there often comes bundled with a genuine cure.
