# ⛪ The Still Hall

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Deity/Deities Worshipped:** [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Aradia]]
- **Type:** Funerary/Scholarly Order
- **Size:** Small, with independent chapters anywhere necromancy is tolerated enough to organize
- **Headquarters:** [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Preopia/Gidor_County/Schaffstal/Schaffstal|Schaffstal]], Gidor County

## Core Beliefs

A body and the soul that left it are something to be managed with care, not mourned with noise. Permanence matters more than sentiment: a properly kept grave, a soul laid to rest exactly once.

## Practices & Rituals

Hall clergy are precise, unhurried necromancers with no patience for anyone who raises the dead carelessly or for spectacle. Hall rites favor quiet, exact work over ceremony.

## Structure & Hierarchy

Where the old [[Pantheon/Religions/The_Last_Vigil|Last Vigil]] once kept watch over the dying, the Still Hall takes over once the watching is done. Its seat is Schaffstal, a lake-and-river trading city in Gidor County not far from Aradia's own origins, its docks and shipping lanes carrying the faith outward the same way they carry everything else the city trades in. Independent chapters have taken root anywhere else necromancy is practiced openly enough to organize around.
