# ⛪ The Gilded Court

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Deity/Deities Worshipped:** [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Galaphorus]]
- **Type:** Courtly Mystery Faith
- **Size:** Small, present in nearly every royal court and salon worth entering
- **Headquarters:** none fixed, hosted wherever a wealthy patron currently keeps it

## Core Beliefs

Being wanted is worth more than being known. Charm and presence are their own kind of power, and worship means practicing exactly what Galaphorus is best at.

## Practices & Rituals

The Court works as a standing invitation rather than a church. Rank comes from charm, not piety, and members compete openly for whoever can hold a room's attention longest. There's no fixed liturgy. The faith is practiced through simply being present, and being magnetic, in courts from Yeripia to Breotrasseon and everywhere ambition and flattery mix.

## Structure & Hierarchy

A loose, shifting circle of courtiers, performers, and the occasional confidence artist, the Court moves with whichever wealthy patron currently hosts it rather than keeping any building of its own. Outsiders often can't tell where the faith ends and simple high society begins, which is precisely how its god prefers it.
