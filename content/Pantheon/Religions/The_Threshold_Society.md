# ⛪ The Threshold Society

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Deity/Deities Worshipped:** [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Solum]]
- **Type:** Travelers'/Conjurers' Order
- **Size:** Small, scattered along every trade route and portal site worth watching
- **Headquarters:** none, membership is a key, not a building

## Core Beliefs

Open doors kept in Solum's name matter more than any temple built to stay in one place. Access and possibility outweigh permanence, and every closed door is worth wondering what was behind it.

## Practices & Rituals

Members carry a small crystal key as their only mark of faith, tuned, they claim, to whichever plane they're most drawn toward. A Society gathering is a meeting point rather than a service, held wherever a promising crossing has opened recently, from the Kalantawi shipping lanes to the mountain passes of Laiqamore, and disbanded the moment it closes.

## Structure & Hierarchy

Its members are smugglers, explorers, and conjurers rather than ordained clergy. The Society keeps no fixed liturgy or standing leadership. It exists wherever its members happen to be, following the same restless logic as the goddess it follows.
