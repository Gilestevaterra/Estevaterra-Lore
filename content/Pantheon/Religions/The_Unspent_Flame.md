# ⛪ The Unspent Flame

**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Deity/Deities Worshipped:** [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Kogeros]]
- **Type:** Practitioners' Faith
- **Size:** Small, fast-growing, with no single home
- **Headquarters:** none fixed, the faith deliberately keeps no permanent hall

## Core Beliefs

A gift sitting unused is a gift wasted, exactly the creed Kogeros lived by even before she was a god. Ambition and progress matter more than caution or tradition, which the Flame sees as just another Old God habit worth burning away.

## Practices & Rituals

Joining is marked by doing something with what you have, not by initiation into a hall: casting a spell that matters, finishing work everyone said couldn't be finished, taking the risk instead of waiting for permission. There is no fixed liturgy or standing ceremony to speak of.

## Structure & Hierarchy

The Flame draws its following from evocation mages, artisans, laborers, and anyone else who refuses to hoard power without using it. It keeps no permanent temple and no standing clergy, on the belief that any institution eventually becomes exactly the kind of hoarded, unused power it exists to burn away. It's the youngest of the New God faiths to take real institutional shape, and the fastest-growing.
