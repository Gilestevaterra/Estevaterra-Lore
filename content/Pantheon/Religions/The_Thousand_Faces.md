**Status:** #draft

## At a Glance

- **Deity/Deities Worshipped:** [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Dheerin]]
- **Type:** Practitioners'/Performers' Faith
- **Size:** Small, popular among alchemists and shapeshifters everywhere
- **Headquarters:** no fixed seat established, could plausibly cluster near [[Geography/Aecrudia/Aecrudia|Aecrudia]] given Dheerin's origin there _(open, confirm or redirect)_

## Core Beliefs

Change itself is worth worshipping, not any single form it takes. A rite repeated the same way twice has already gone stale.

## Practices & Rituals

Followers (transmutation alchemists, actors, more than a few shapeshifters) mark devotion by how convincingly they can become something they weren't a moment before. The faith keeps no fixed liturgy, and a Thousand Faces gathering can look entirely different from one year to the next by design.

## Structure & Hierarchy

Organized in troupes and workshops that turn up wherever the craft is practiced rather than around any one home base. No formal ordination exists. Standing among the Faces comes from skill at transformation itself.
