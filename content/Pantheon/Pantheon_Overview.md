# 🏛️ Pantheon Overview

**Status:** #draft / #reviewed

> _The complete divine picture: who the gods are, how they relate, and what they mean to the world._

## The Nature of the Gods

The Old Gods created Estevaterra. Each held domain over an element (or an elemental concept, in the case of Life and Death) and ruled directly over a physical part of the world, in addition to that domain. They are not "good" or "evil" in any conventional sense, they simply are, and were seen by different people as saviors or as cruel despots depending on where those people stood. In the time of their rule, magic did not exist except as something the gods doled out directly: fantastical objects and creatures were their creations alone, and such creation was rare. They cannot be casually killed by mortals: the only way an Old God has ever been overcome is the ritual described under [[#The Divine Divide]] below, and even then they were not destroyed, only cast out of this plane of existence (see [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Vegran]], who is actively working to return).

The New Gods are of an entirely different nature: they are transformed mortals, not a separate order of being that predates the world. See below.

## The Old Gods

Six Old Gods are currently on record: [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Avaretal]] (Fire), [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Faranaha]] (Life), [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Haviktla]] (Water), [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Mercaraz]] (Air), [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Mgellzir]] (Earth), and [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Vegran]] (Death). Each also ruled a specific physical region of the world alongside their elemental domain.

The world's landmasses have been divided among the six by physical geography (continents, mountain ranges, rivers, and lakes: not by the current political map, which came later and can straddle these divine boundaries). Each Old God's own page has the details under its own Canonical Lands section; summarized here:

- [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Faranaha]] — [[Political_Divisions/Crucaea|Crucaea]]
- [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Mgellzir]] — [[Political_Divisions/Laiqamore|Laiqamore]]
- [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Vegran]] — western [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Kingdom_of_Yeripia|Yeripia]] (chosen to include [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yeripia/Preopia/Gidor_County/Barony_of_Mag_Beoga/Lostshade/Lostshade|Lostshade]])
- [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Mercaraz]] — eastern Yeripia and [[Political_Divisions/Breotrasseon|Breotrasseon]]
- [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Haviktla]] — [[Geography/Aecrudia/Aecrudia|Aecrudia]] and the [[Political_Divisions/Kingdom_of_Yahan|Kingdom of Yahan]]
- [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Avaretal]] — the [[Slaecaea_Islands|Slaecaea Islands]], [[Vraediris|Isle of Vraediris]], [[Geography/Sea_of_Leeronto/Haidonia|Haidonia]], and the scattered western islands

Over the centuries of the Old Gods' rule, their blood (the literal source of their creative and magical power) slowly seeped into the world. A drop here and there from acts of creation accumulated in the soil over generations, forming natural low-magic concentrations that entered the food and water supply and began changing the humans and animals that consumed it. Dwarves and elves are the most visible result of this slow contamination. Less intelligent creatures were affected too, producing things like dire wolves and owlbears that arose on their own rather than through direct divine creation. At the time of the world's founding, humans were the only sentient species, living alongside ordinary Earth-like beasts, everything else described above emerged gradually, becoming less rare as the centuries passed but never common while the Old Gods still ruled directly.

## The New Gods

By the time the Old Gods were cast out, this ambient magic had begun to manifest directly in a small number of individuals, most of whom didn't yet understand what it was. The New Gods are the people from that generation who took a further step: see [[#The Divine Divide]] below for how. Eight New Gods are currently on record, and each is tied to one of the eight schools of magic rather than an element: [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Aradia]] (Necromancy), [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Dheerin]] (Transmutation), [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Dophy]] (Divination), [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Galaphorus]] (Enchantment), [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Kogeros]] (Evocation), [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Solum]] (Conjuration), [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Valtor]] (Abjuration), and [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Xyzdia]] (Illusion). Unlike the Old Gods, each New God was once an ordinary mortal (several of their pages, e.g. [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Dophy]], still show traces of their original human face or form): their transformation reshaped them into something unique to that individual, rather than granting a shared "divine template." _(Needs Review: which Old God's fall produced which specific New God(s) is only confirmed for one pairing, Kogeros and Avaretal, see below. There were 6 Old Gods and 8 New Gods, so at least one ritual against an Old God produced more than one New God. The rest of the mapping is an open question for future modules.)_

## The Divine Divide

While the Old Gods were not evil, many mortals found their rule oppressive and yearned to be free of it. A woman who would go on to become [[Pantheon/New_Gods/Kogeros]] (_her name before this is not yet known. Shane has noted it may surface in a future OneNote batch_) was among those in whom the ambient magic had begun to manifest, and she learned to use her power to devise a ritual capable of expelling an Old God from this plane of existence entirely. The ritual required spilling a great quantity of the target god's blood and drenching herself in it, absorbing its power in the same act that cast the god out. She used it first against [[Pantheon/Old_Gods/Avaretal]], see [[History/Events/The_Fall_of_Avaretal]] for the founding event itself. Once it was shown to work, the same approach was used against each of the other Old Gods in turn (see [[History/Events/The_Fall_of_Faranaha]], [[History/Events/The_Fall_of_Haviktla]], [[History/Events/The_Fall_of_Mercaraz]], [[History/Events/The_Fall_of_Mgellzir]], and [[History/Events/The_Fall_of_Vegran]]).

Nobody involved understood the consequences at the time. Anyone who drowned in that much divine blood was permanently transformed by it rather than simply borrowing the power for the ritual: this is how the New Gods came to exist, each changed differently, their unique powers aligning to what are now the schools of magic. There was a second, larger consequence nobody could have anticipated: the Old Gods had been shielding Estevaterra from the wider universe, keeping the world hidden from anything beyond it. When they were cast out, that shield fell with them, and Estevaterra became visible (and reachable) to creatures from other planes for the first time.

## Divine Influence on the World

The dropped shield is the direct cause of Estevaterra's current central conflict: devils, demons, and other extraplanar creatures can now reach the world, and by the present year (231 NG) they have become a real and growing problem, see [[Magic/Systems/Origins_of_Magic]] for the full arc from low magic to the current day. Politically and socially, the world is caught in a transition from low magic to high magic: magic is becoming more common but most people remain unused to it, and fear is a frequent reaction. Open use of magic on another person, or in a way that affects someone else, is officially illegal, though authorities generally look the other way when it's used in self-defense or to help someone in need (such as healing).
